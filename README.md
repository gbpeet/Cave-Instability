# Cave-Instability

> **Dig carefully—every block you remove could bring the cave down around you.**

Cave-Instability transforms Minecraft's perfectly stable caves into dangerous underground environments where unsupported rock can collapse, debris can cascade downhill, and floating formations can crash to the ground. Every mining expedition becomes a careful balance between progress and survival.

---

## Features

- 🪨 Realistic cave-ins and ceiling collapses
- ⛰️ Floating rock formations lose support and fall
- 🌋 Debris naturally slides downhill during large collapses
- 🏗️ Player-built support pillars stabilize dangerous caves
- ⚙️ Fully configurable through Mod Menu
- 🧱 Compatible with both vanilla and modded blocks
- 💨 Dust particles and immersive collapse sounds
- ⚡ Optimized for survival gameplay with minimal performance impact

---

## How It Works

Every time you mine, Cave-Instability evaluates the surrounding terrain for structural stability.

Unsupported blocks may begin to fall, triggering larger collapses that can propagate through weakened rock. Massive cave-ins can send debris sliding across cave floors, dramatically reshaping underground spaces.

Building support pillars from configured materials allows players to reinforce unstable caverns and safely continue mining.

---

## Configuration

Nearly every aspect of Cave-Instability can be customized through Mod Menu, including:

- Collapse probability
- Collapse search radius
- Collapse propagation strength
- Debris sliding
- Structural support pillars
- Support materials
- Dust particle intensity
- Sound effects
